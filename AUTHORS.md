## GNOME Screenshot

Videoshot is a fork of GNOME Screenshot 41.0. Its authors, as listed by the
application and its source files:

- Jonathan Blandford
- Emmanuele Bassi
- Cosimo Cecchi
- Alexander Mikhaylenko
- Philipp Wolfer
- Nils Dagsson Moskopp
- Tobias Bernard and Jakub Steiner (artwork)

Many more people contributed code and translations. The git history and
[NEWS](NEWS) name them.

## Linux Mint

The fork starts from Linux Mint's `gnome-screenshot 41.0+mint2`, with changes
by Clement Lefebvre and Michael Webster on top of the Debian packaging.
